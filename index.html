The Art of Writing Clean Markup

Published on October 24, 2026 by Developer

Writing **"good" HTML code** means focusing on three core pillars: readability, accessibility, and search engine optimization (SEO). By choosing elements based on their meaning rather than their visual look, you build a resilient skeleton for the web.

Core Implementation Principles

  • Semantic Layouts: Swap generic divs for structured components like <header>, <nav>, <main>, and <footer>.
  • Rigid Hierarchy: Keep heading tags structural (<h1> through <h6>) instead of using them for font resizing.
  • Implicit Accessibility: Always attach alt text to descriptive images and link <label> nodes directly to form controls.
